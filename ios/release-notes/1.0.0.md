# Cold Gas 1.0.0

Frost Season: The Director's Cut

The nights are long and the air is cold, which is just how cold-gas jets like it. Since the first build, the iPhone got the web game's cameras, its night sky and faster thumbs.

## Highlights
- **Lights, camera, liftoff.** The web game's camera crew: hard cuts, a held wide and the cut to a wreck. <!-- icon: video -->
- **A clear night out.** Stars, the launch complex, a wet slab that shines and the jets' mist. <!-- icon: sparkles -->
- **Speedrun thumbs.** Touches reach the jets at once, with no wait at the screen's edge. <!-- icon: hand.tap -->
- **The short version.** How to play fits a glance, and a tap on a judge's score reads their call. <!-- icon: person.3 -->

## What is in this build
- **Three courses, flown live.** The Show, the Wall and the Lineup, on the same physics as the web game, bit for bit.
- **Three ways to fly.** Assist on one thumb, Pro on a stick and buttons, Advanced with all ten valves by hand. A controller or a keyboard works too.
- **The results.** The score in a bright field, the three judges and their words a tap away, what cost you points, medals, the daily jump, the board, a challenge link, and your flight played back.
- **Practice.** Drills for each part of the flight.
- **Sound and haptics.** Both follow the sim, and both can be turned down in Settings.
- **iPhone Duo.** Open: edge to edge. Tabletop: the top half is the screen and the bottom half the controller.

## What to test
- Open How to play, then the results after a flight. Is the important thing obvious at a glance? Tap a judge's score to read their call.
- Tell us what you'd change. What you send shapes the next build.
- Fly the Show on one thumb: hold to climb, tap on the ledge, hold to land.
- Crash on purpose. The car should dent, throw sparks and roll to rest before the results come up.
- Post a run with three letters and find it on the board. Posts are real and public.
- Leave the app mid-run and come back: it should be paused, with nothing held.
- On an iPhone Duo, fold and unfold it mid-run.
- Anything that crashes, stutters or looks wrong: send it from TestFlight with a screenshot.
