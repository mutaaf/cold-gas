# Cold Gas 1.1.0

The Judges Deliberate Behind Closed Doors

Like every good talent-show panel, the judges now confer in private: on an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence, they write their verdict on the device itself. Nothing leaves it and it costs nothing, which leaves the long November nights for one more run.

## Highlights
- **Judged on device.** With Apple Intelligence, the panel writes its words on your iPhone. No network, $0. <!-- icon: cpu, go: settings -->
- **Free Drive.** Off the Show's apron and out into a world that goes on. Unscored, from the title. <!-- icon: car -->
- **Controls that fit.** Pro and Advanced are shaped for the screen: phone, Duo open or tabletop, iPad. <!-- icon: gamecontroller -->

## What is in this build
- **The judges use Apple Intelligence.** On an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence turned on (iOS 26 or later), the three judges write their words with Apple's on-device model. The run's numbers are all it sees; nothing about the run is sent anywhere, and it costs nothing. The scores are the game's own, as always: the model only writes the words.
- **Honest about who spoke.** Under the judges, a small "Judged on device" mark and the receipt: Apple's on-device model, the tokens it used, the time, $0, and that no data left the device.
- **Everywhere else, nothing changes.** Without Apple Intelligence, or if the on-device model can't answer, the free edge model writes the words over the network, and if that can't either, the game's own lines say so.
- **Your choice.** Settings, Judges: see which brain judges on this device, and send the panel to the edge model instead if you prefer.
- **Free Drive.** Leave the Show's apron and drive on, in a world built as you go. Nothing is scored.
- **Pro and Advanced controls** sit under your thumbs on a phone held either way, the Duo open or in tabletop, and an iPad.

## What to test
- On a device with Apple Intelligence: fly any course and watch the judges' words land. Does the receipt say Apple's on-device model, on this iPhone, $0? Turn on Airplane Mode and fly again: the judges should still speak.
- Settings, Judges: turn "Judge on this device" off, fly, and check the receipt names the edge model instead.
- On a device without Apple Intelligence: the judges should speak exactly as before, from the edge model.
- Try Free Drive from the title, and Pro or Advanced on your phone both ways up.

## Our promises
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. The judges' words on the device never leave it.
