# Cold Gas 1.2.0

Adjust Your Seat and Mirrors

Every film test pilot slides the seat forward before the big flight. Now you can too, and like a crew that leaves the set standing after a wrap, the sets stay open to roam.

## Highlights
- **Your controls, your way.** Drag, pinch and fade every button; presets, a mirror and stick modes. <!-- icon: rectangle.and.hand.point.up.left, go: settings -->
- **Roam the sets.** Drive the Show, the Wall and the Lineup between runs, then out into the world. <!-- icon: car.side -->
- **Three looks.** Real, Anime or 8-bit: pick one in Settings, scores untouched. <!-- icon: paintpalette, go: settings -->
- **Wreck Club.** Every crash scores Wreck too: combos, feats, ranks, new paint. <!-- icon: flag.checkered, go: club -->

## What is in this build
- **A first jump that teaches itself.** A fresh install opens on the mark: tap to fly, one hint per beat, a moment for the first landing.
- **Next, then beat it.** After a landing: Next (the next event), then Beat your score and Share; Post sits below.
- **Share the moment.** A picture and a link to beat it; challenge links open in the app.
- **Three looks.** Settings, Look: Real, Anime (cel bands, ink, a moonlit painted sky, speed lines) or 8-bit (pixel art: 32 colours, outlines). A steadier frame.
- **The HUD layout editor.** Settings, Controls, Edit the layout: drag, resize, fade, snap guides you feel, Undo, Reset.
- **A layout per grip.** Per tier and shape: upright, sideways, the Duo, iPad.
- **Presets and the stick.** Thumbs, Claw, Left-handed, Minimal, Mirror; a fixed, floating or following stick.
- **Roam.** Under Fly and on the results: the set and the world beyond, unscored; Fly starts a scored run.
- **Loose things.** Cones and signs move at their weights; fences and rails break.
- **A road car's chassis.** Roaming: tyres that slide, springs, lean, ABS, Stability, hot discs.
- **Paint, lamps, a cinema camera.** Clear coat, brake lamps; cinema by the road.
- **A world that looks made.** New trees and boulders, smooth cuttings, a map that turns.
- **Autopilot**, downforce, calmer reds roaming.
- **The moment, again.** Each landing and wreck replays slowed before the card, felt in step; tap to skip.
- **Sound, rebuilt.** All made live: motors, tyres, ABS, wind, the jets, landings, hits, the crowd in 3D. Effects and Music sliders.
- **Never stuck on a pole.** A wedged run restarts from the mark; a wedged roaming car goes back on the road.
- **Usage stats, private.** A few counts, never mid-run; Settings, Privacy turns them off.
- **The crew.** Hollis, Mags and Vee judge each run in their own words, from your numbers, offline; Apple Intelligence rewrites them where it can.
- **Wreck Club.** A second score from the crash's physics, combos called and felt, never added to your score. Feats raise your rank and unlock paints.
- **Nothing scored changed.** Scores, the stunt physics and your recorded runs are exactly as they were.

## What to test
- Delete and reinstall: does a tap fly at once? Do the hints help? Land it: does the moment feel earned?
- Edit the layout: move, resize, fade, Save, fly, reopen. Try presets, Mirror, Undo, Reset.
- Pro and Advanced on the Wall with a custom layout, past the ledge to the landing.
- Roam the Lineup into the row: does it shove and dent? Share a run to Messages and tap the link.
- Settings, Look: fly and roam in Anime and 8-bit. Smooth? Is the HUD easy to read?
- Steer hard left from the start into a set light: "Stuck", then again from the mark?
- Free Drive: throw the car into a bend, brake hard: does it lean, do the discs glow?
- Settings, Privacy: stats and crash reports off, fly, back on.
- Settings, Judges: turn Judge on this device off, fly: the crew speaks at once.
- Crash on purpose: do combos show and buzz? Earn a feat, then try a paint in Wreck Club.

## Our promises
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Usage stats are private and one switch turns them off; your layouts stay on your device.
