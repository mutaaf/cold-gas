# Cold Gas 1.2.0

Adjust Your Seat and Mirrors

Every film test pilot slides the seat forward before the big flight. Now you can too: put every button where your hands want it, and make them bigger for cold November thumbs.

## Highlights
- **Your controls, your way.** Drag, pinch and fade every button and the stick. Saved per tier and per screen. <!-- icon: rectangle.and.hand.point.up.left, go: settings -->
- **Presets and a mirror.** Thumbs, Claw for three or four fingers, Left-handed, and Minimal, in one tap. <!-- icon: square.grid.2x2 -->
- **A stick that follows.** Fixed, floating or follows the thumb, with dead zone and sensitivity. <!-- icon: dot.circle.and.hand.point.up.left.fill -->
- **Tap to hold.** BRAKE and BOOST can latch on a tap, and RELEASE can fire as your finger lifts. <!-- icon: hand.tap -->

## What is in this build
- **The HUD layout editor.** Settings, Controls, Edit the layout, or Edit controls on the pause sheet. Drag any control anywhere, pinch it (or use the Size slider) to resize it, and give it its own opacity. Lines show where it snaps to the middle, the edges and the other controls, with a tick you can feel. Overlaps are flagged. Undo steps back; Reset puts the factory layout back.
- **One layout for each way you hold it.** Assist, Pro, Advanced and Free Drive each keep their own layout, for a phone upright or sideways, the Duo closed, open (book or flat) or in tabletop, and an iPad. Nothing can be placed across the Duo's fold: a control dropped on it lands beside it.
- **Presets.** Thumbs (the factory), Claw (index fingers on the upper buttons), Left-handed (the stick under the right thumb) and Minimal (smaller and fainter). Mirror flips whatever you have. If you chose the left hand before, the mirror is where you start.
- **The stick.** Fixed, floating (the ring comes to a thumb that lands beside it) or follows the thumb anywhere on its side of the screen. Dead zone and sensitivity sliders.
- **Free Drive.** Swipe anywhere off the controls to look round the car; it drifts back behind you when you let go. Sensitivity and invert in Settings.
- **The buttons.** Tap BRAKE or BOOST to hold them, tap again to let go. RELEASE can let go of the Wall as your finger lifts, so you can rest on it and choose the moment.
- **Nothing about the game changed.** The controls only change where your fingers land. Scores, the physics and your recorded runs are exactly as they were. A controller and a keyboard fly as before.
- **VoiceOver** names every control in the editor, with actions to move, size and fade it.

## What to test
- Settings, Controls, Edit the layout: drag the stick and a button, pinch one bigger, fade one, Save. Fly a run with it. Quit the app and open it again: is the layout still yours?
- Try each preset and Mirror, then Undo and Reset.
- Turn the phone sideways (or open the Duo): you get that shape's own layout. On a Duo, try to drop a button on the fold.
- Pro and Advanced on the Wall with a custom layout, all the way past the ledge to the landing.
- Free Drive: swipe on empty glass to look round.
