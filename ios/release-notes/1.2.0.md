# Cold Gas 1.2.0

Adjust Your Seat and Mirrors

Every film test pilot slides the seat forward before the big flight. Now you can too: put every button where your hands want it, and make them bigger for cold November thumbs. And like a stunt crew that leaves the set standing after a wrap, the Show, the Wall and the Lineup stay open for you to roam.

## What is in this build
- **The HUD layout editor.** Settings, Controls, Edit the layout, or Edit controls on the pause sheet. Drag any control anywhere, pinch it (or use the Size slider) to resize it, and give it its own opacity. Lines show where it snaps to the middle, the edges and the other controls, with a tick you can feel. Overlaps are flagged. Undo steps back; Reset puts the factory layout back.
- **One layout for each way you hold it.** Assist, Pro, Advanced and Free Drive each keep their own layout, for a phone upright or sideways, the Duo closed, open (book or flat) or in tabletop, and an iPad. Nothing can be placed across the Duo's fold: a control dropped on it lands beside it.
- **Presets.** Thumbs (the factory), Claw (index fingers on the upper buttons), Left-handed (the stick under the right thumb) and Minimal (smaller and fainter). Mirror flips whatever you have. If you chose the left hand before, the mirror is where you start.
- **The stick.** Fixed, floating (the ring comes to a thumb that lands beside it) or follows the thumb anywhere on its side of the screen. Dead zone and sensitivity sliders.
- **Free Drive.** Swipe anywhere off the controls to look round the car; it drifts back behind you when you let go. Sensitivity and invert in Settings.
- **The buttons.** Tap BRAKE or BOOST to hold them, tap again to let go. RELEASE can let go of the Wall as your finger lifts, so you can rest on it and choose the moment.
- **Roam.** Beside Fly on the title, and on the results sheet after a run: the course you chose, its parked row, its pad and its lights, with Free Drive's open world beyond it in every direction. Nothing is scored. The jets fly and the tanks refill on the ground, and the fan holds you to the Wall's curl. "Fly" under Back takes you straight into a scored run from the mark. Free Drive itself is the globe button.
- **Loose things.** Each set has cones, water-fill barriers and A-frame signs that move as their real weights do: a cone leaves faster than the car that hit it, and a barrier skids and tips.
- **Marks on the ground.** A sliding or spinning tyre lays rubber on the concrete and the roads, and it stays there. Smoke rises off a hard slide; gravel, dirt, sand and grass are thrown up behind you.
- **Felt.** Impacts shake the camera and the phone in your hand, scaled by how hard you hit. Rough ground and a slide rumble under them. Back on the road makes the car whole again.
- **A better autopilot and a planted car.** Free Drive's autopilot keeps to its lane through bends, and the car presses down a little harder at speed.
- **Nothing about the game changed.** The controls only change where your fingers land, and a roam is never scored. Scores, the stunt physics and your recorded runs are exactly as they were. A controller and a keyboard fly as before.
- **VoiceOver** names every control in the editor, with actions to move, size and fade it.

## What to test
- Settings, Controls, Edit the layout: drag the stick and a button, pinch one bigger, fade one, Save. Fly a run with it. Quit the app and open it again: is the layout still yours?
- Try each preset and Mirror, then Undo and Reset.
- Turn the phone sideways (or open the Duo): you get that shape's own layout. On a Duo, try to drop a button on the fold.
- Pro and Advanced on the Wall with a custom layout, all the way past the ledge to the landing.
- Free Drive: swipe on empty glass to look round.
- Pick the Lineup, tap Roam, and drive into the parked row. Does it shove, and does your car dent? Tap Fly: does a scored run start from the mark?
- On the Show's apron, run the slalom and the cones round the pad. Hold full lock and full throttle on the concrete: do the marks stay?
- Drive off the apron onto grass and gravel: dust behind the wheels?
