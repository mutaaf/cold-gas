## What is in this build
- **The HUD layout editor.** Settings, Controls, Edit the layout: drag, resize and fade, with snap guides you can feel, Undo and Reset.
- **A layout for each way you hold it.** Each tier keeps one per shape: phone upright or sideways, the Duo's poses, iPad.
- **Presets.** Thumbs, Claw, Left-handed and Minimal, and a Mirror for any of them.
- **The stick and buttons.** Fixed, floating or follow-the-thumb stick with dead zone and sensitivity. BRAKE and BOOST can latch on a tap; RELEASE can fire as your finger lifts.
- **Roam.** Beside Fly on the title and on the results sheet: the course's set, with Free Drive's world beyond it. Unscored. The jets fly, the tanks refill, the fan holds you to the curl, and Fly takes you straight into a scored run. Free Drive is the globe button; swipe off the controls to look round.
- **Loose things and marks.** Cones, barriers and signs move as their real weights do; out in the world, fences, bend signs and guard rails break loose when hit hard. Tyres lay rubber that stays; slides smoke, and dirt and grass fly.
- **Felt.** Impacts shake the camera and the phone; rough ground, slides and ABS buzz. Back on the road repairs the car.
- **A road car's chassis.** Like the stunt double who drives between the stunts, the roaming car has its own: springs you see working, a body that leans and dives, rear motors steering power to the outside wheel, ABS, and discs that glow after hard stops.
- **Paint, lamps, plate.** A deep clear coat that carries the dusk, brake lamps on the pedal, and a plate that reads the right way round.
- **A cinema camera.** The camera button goes chase, cinema, bumper: cinema waits by the road and watches you pass.
- **Calmer sets.** Roaming the Show or the Wall, the reds are turned down.
- **A world that looks made.** New trees and boulders, smooth cuttings, and a map that turns with you.
- **A real sky.** Like the crew that waits all day for the magic-hour shot, Free Drive starts at golden hour: a physical sky, long shadows, haze that turns the ranges blue, worn asphalt, swaying grass. The sun button picks the hour, or lets the day run.
- **A lane-keeping autopilot**, and downforce.
- **Nothing scored changed.** Scores, the stunt physics and your recorded runs are exactly as they were.

## What to test
- Edit the layout: move, resize and fade controls, Save, fly a run, reopen the app. Try the presets, Mirror, Undo, Reset, and another shape.
- Pro and Advanced on the Wall with a custom layout, past the ledge to the landing.
- Roam the Lineup into the parked row: does it shove and dent? Tap Fly: does a scored run start?
- On the Show's apron, run the cones; hold full lock and throttle: do the marks stay? Off onto grass: dust?
- In Free Drive, throw the car into a bend: does it lean? Brake hard from speed, then the cinema camera: do the discs glow?
- Tap the sun button through every hour. Does each read? Do the lamps come on after sunset?
