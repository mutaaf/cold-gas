# Cold Gas 1.2.0

Adjust Your Seat and Mirrors

Every film test pilot slides the seat forward before the big flight. Now you can too, and like a crew that leaves the set standing after a wrap, the sets stay open to roam.

## Highlights
- **Your controls, your way.** Drag, pinch and fade every button; presets, a mirror and stick modes. <!-- icon: rectangle.and.hand.point.up.left, go: settings -->
- **Roam the sets.** Drive the Show, the Wall and the Lineup between runs, then out into the world. <!-- icon: car.side -->
- **A first jump, taught.** One tap to fly, a hint at each beat, a landing you feel. <!-- icon: hand.tap -->
- **Hear the road.** An electric drivetrain, tyres by ground, hits by material, sound in 3D. <!-- icon: speaker.wave.3, go: settings -->

## What is in this build
- **A first jump that teaches itself.** A fresh install opens on the mark: tap to fly, one hint per beat, a moment for the first landing.
- **Next, then beat it.** After a landing: Next (the next event), then Beat your score and Share; Post sits below.
- **Share the moment.** A picture of the jump and a link to beat it; wrecks too.
- **Challenge links open in the app.** A friend's link opens on their card: Take the challenge, then Send it back.
- **The HUD layout editor.** Settings, Controls, Edit the layout: drag, resize, fade, snap guides you feel, Undo, Reset.
- **A layout for each way you hold it.** Per tier and per shape: upright, sideways, the Duo's poses, iPad.
- **Presets and the stick.** Thumbs, Claw, Left-handed, Minimal, Mirror; a fixed, floating or following stick; latching BRAKE and BOOST.
- **Roam.** Under Fly and on the results: the set and the world beyond, unscored; Fly starts a scored run.
- **Loose things and marks.** Cones and signs move at their weights; fences and rails break when hit hard. Rubber stays, dirt flies.
- **Felt.** Impacts shake the camera and the phone; slides and ABS buzz.
- **A road car's chassis.** Roaming: springs, lean and dive, ABS, torque vectoring, glowing discs. The stunt car is unchanged.
- **Paint, lamps, a cinema camera.** Clear coat, brake lamps on the pedal; cinema waits by the road.
- **A world that looks made.** New trees and boulders, smooth cuttings, a map that turns.
- **A lane-keeping autopilot**, downforce at speed, calmer reds when roaming.
- **Sound, rebuilt.** Like a foley stage, all made live: motors whining with load and regen, tyres that roar, crunch, swish or squeal, ABS, wind, the jets' punch and hiss, landings by how hard, hits by material, birds and the crowd round you in 3D. Effects and Music sliders.
- **Never stuck on a pole.** A run wedged against a set light starts again from the mark; out roaming, a wedged car goes back on the road.
- **Usage stats, private.** A few counts (started, landed, shared) to our own relay with a random id, never mid-run; Settings, Privacy turns them off.
- **Nothing scored changed.** Scores, the stunt physics and your recorded runs are exactly as they were.

## What to test
- Delete and reinstall: does a tap fly at once? Do the hints help? Land it: does the moment feel earned?
- Edit the layout: move, resize, fade, Save, fly, reopen. Try presets, Mirror, Undo, Reset.
- Pro and Advanced on the Wall with a custom layout, past the ledge to the landing.
- Roam the Lineup into the row: does it shove and dent? Share a run to Messages and tap the link.
- On the Show's apron, run the cones; full lock and throttle: do the marks stay?
- Steer a run hard left from the start into a set light: after a second, "Stuck", then again from the mark?
- In Free Drive, throw the car into a bend: does it lean? Brake hard a few times: do the discs glow?
- Settings, Privacy: turn Share usage stats off, fly, turn it on again.
- With headphones, drive onto gravel and grass, past trees, into a fence: does each sound right?

## Our promises
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Usage stats are private and one switch turns them off; your layouts stay on your device.
