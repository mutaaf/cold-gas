# Cold Gas 1.2.0

Adjust Your Seat and Mirrors

Every film test pilot slides the seat forward before the big flight. Now you can too, and like a crew that leaves the set standing after a wrap, the sets stay open to roam.

## Highlights
- **Your controls, your way.** Drag, pinch and fade every button; presets, a mirror and stick modes. <!-- icon: rectangle.and.hand.point.up.left, go: settings -->
- **Roam the sets.** Drive the Show, the Wall and the Lineup between runs, then out into the world. <!-- icon: car.side -->
- **Three looks.** Real, Anime or 8-bit: pick one in Settings, scores untouched. <!-- icon: paintpalette, go: settings -->
- **Wreck Club.** Every crash scores Wreck too: combos, feats, ranks, new paint. <!-- icon: flag.checkered, go: club -->

## What is in this build
- **A first jump that teaches itself.** A fresh install opens on the mark: tap to fly, one hint per beat, a moment for the first landing.
- **Next, then beat it.** After a landing: Next, Beat your score, Share.
- **Share the moment.** A picture and a link to beat it; challenge links open in the app.
- **Three looks.** Real, Anime (cel, ink, speed lines) or 8-bit (32-colour pixel art).
- **The HUD layout editor.** Settings, Controls: drag, resize, fade, felt snap guides, Undo.
- **A layout per grip.** Per tier and shape: upright, sideways, the Duo, iPad.
- **Presets and the stick.** Thumbs, Claw, Left-handed, Minimal, Mirror; a fixed, floating or following stick.
- **Roam.** Under Fly and on the results, unscored: topple tyres, set challenges.
- **Water you can drive into.** Sea, lakes and rivers: wade, float, flood, drift downstream.
- **Everything leaves a mark.** Ruts, mud on the car, shattered glass; trees snap.
- **A road car's chassis.** Sliding tyres, springs, lean, ABS, hot discs.
- **Grip, slides, stops.** Handbrake, drift assist, brake fade, skid marks.
- **Paint, lamps, a cinema camera.** Clear coat, brake lamps, downforce.
- **Every vehicle, redrawn.** Roadster, lineup, launch site.
- **Sets that look built.** Worn concrete, crew paint, trees, boulders.
- **A world that looks real.** A real sky at any hour, rock, snow, wet shores, swaying trees that cast shade.
- **The moment, again.** Landings and wrecks replay slowed before the card, felt in step; tap to skip.
- **Sound, rebuilt.** Live motors, tyres, wind, jets, hits, a 3D crowd; volume sliders.
- **No glare at the start.** No haze on the car.
- **Lighter.** Less memory, fewer frames when idle.
- **Never stuck on a pole.** A wedged run or roam restarts.
- **Usage stats, private.** A few counts, never mid-run; off in Settings.
- **The crew.** Hollis, Mags and Vee judge each run offline; Apple Intelligence rewrites where it can.
- **Wreck Club.** A second score from the crash, never added to yours: combos, feats, ranks.
- **Splash and sink.** Tanks and mud on the Show and Lineup; old runs replay as flown.

## What to test
- Reinstall: does a tap fly at once? Do the hints help? Does the landing feel earned?
- Edit the layout: move, resize, fade, Save, fly, reopen; presets, Undo.
- Pro and Advanced on the Wall, custom layout, past the ledge to the landing.
- Roam: topple the Show's tyres, shove the Lineup's row: dents? Share a run to Messages, tap the link.
- Fly and roam in Anime and 8-bit: smooth? Is the HUD easy to read?
- Free Drive: the sun button through the hours; into a lake, a river or the sea: wade, float, sink?
- Free Drive: brake hard in a bend: lean, glowing discs? Spin in mud: ruts, a muddy car?
- Steer hard left into a set light: "Stuck", then again from the mark?
- Settings, Privacy: stats off, fly, back on.
- Land wide of the Show's pad: a splash, a float?
- Settings, Judges: Judge on this device off, fly: the crew speaks at once.
- Crash on purpose: combos shown and felt? Earn a feat, try a paint.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Usage stats are private, one switch stops them; your layouts stay on your device.
