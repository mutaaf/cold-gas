# Cold Gas 1.2.0

Adjust Your Seat and Mirrors

Every film test pilot slides the seat forward before the big flight. Now you can too: put every button where your hands want it. And like a stunt crew that leaves the set standing after a wrap, the Show, the Wall and the Lineup stay open for you to roam, in a car built for the road.

## Highlights
- **Your controls, your way.** Drag, pinch and fade every button; presets, a mirror and stick modes. <!-- icon: rectangle.and.hand.point.up.left, go: settings -->
- **Roam the sets.** Drive the Show, the Wall and the Lineup between runs, then out into the world. <!-- icon: car.side -->
- **A real chassis under you.** It rolls, dives and squats, ABS buzzes, and hard stops make the brakes glow. <!-- icon: gauge.with.dots.needle.67percent -->
- **The world takes the hits.** Fences snap, rails give, cones fly and rubber stays; you feel each knock. <!-- icon: burst -->

## What is in this build
- **The HUD layout editor.** Settings, Controls, Edit the layout: drag, resize and fade, with snap guides you can feel, Undo and Reset.
- **A layout for each way you hold it.** Each tier keeps one per shape: phone upright or sideways, the Duo's poses, iPad.
- **Presets.** Thumbs, Claw, Left-handed and Minimal, and a Mirror for any of them.
- **The stick and buttons.** Fixed, floating or follow-the-thumb stick, dead zone, sensitivity; BRAKE and BOOST can latch, RELEASE can fire on lift.
- **Roam.** Under Fly and on the results: the course's set with the world beyond, unscored. The jets fly, the tanks refill, and Fly starts a scored run. Swipe off the controls to look round.
- **Loose things and marks.** Cones, barriers and signs move as their weights do; fences and rails break loose when hit hard. Rubber stays, slides smoke, dirt flies.
- **Felt.** Impacts shake the camera and the phone; rough ground, slides and ABS buzz. Back on the road repairs the car.
- **A road car's chassis.** The roaming car has springs you see working, a body that leans and dives, ABS, torque to the outside wheel, and discs that glow after hard stops. The stunt car is unchanged.
- **Paint, lamps, plate.** A deep clear coat that carries the dusk, brake lamps on the pedal, and a plate that reads the right way round.
- **A cinema camera.** The camera button goes chase, cinema, bumper: cinema waits by the road and watches you pass.
- **Calmer sets.** Roaming the Show or the Wall, the reds are turned down so the car and the set read.
- **A world that looks made.** New trees, shrubs and boulders, smooth road cuttings, a fuller dusk, and a map that turns with you.
- **A lane-keeping autopilot** and downforce at speed.
- **Never stuck on a pole.** A run wedged against a set light starts again from the mark; out roaming, a wedged car goes back on the road.
- **Usage stats, private.** A few counts (a jump started, landed or shared) go to our own relay with a random id made on the phone, never during a run. Settings, Privacy turns them off.
- **Nothing scored changed.** Scores, the stunt physics and your recorded runs are exactly as they were.

## What to test
- Edit the layout: move, resize and fade controls, Save, fly a run, reopen the app. Try the presets, Mirror, Undo, Reset, and another shape.
- Pro and Advanced on the Wall with a custom layout, past the ledge to the landing.
- Roam the Lineup into the parked row: does it shove and dent? Tap Fly: does a scored run start?
- On the Show's apron, run the cones; hold full lock and throttle: do the marks stay? Off onto grass: dust?
- Steer a run hard left from the start into a set light: after a second, "Stuck", then again from the mark?
- In Free Drive, throw the car into a bend: does it lean? Brake hard a few times: do the discs glow?
- Settings, Privacy: turn Share usage stats off, fly, turn it on again.

## Our promises
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Usage stats are private and one switch turns them off; your layouts stay on your device.
