One jump, made playable. A roadster climbs a curved wall on suction, lets go upside down, fires ten cold-gas thrusters to clear a row of parked cars and lands on the mark. Three judges score it.

THE JUMP
The physics is a deterministic rigid-body sim at 240 ticks a second: raycast suspension, tyre grip, ten nozzles that run their tank down, and a flight computer. The same inputs always fly the same jump, on the phone and on the web.

THREE WAYS TO FLY
- Assist: one thumb. Hold to climb, tap to let go, hold to land. The computer rolls you out and brakes, and a slide of your thumb aims the touchdown.
- Pro: a stick and buttons. You fly the roll and the landing yourself.
- Advanced: all ten valves by hand.
A game controller or a keyboard works too.

THREE COURSES
The Show, the Wall and the Lineup, with drills for each part of the flight and a coach that names what cost you points.

SCORED, NOT GUESSED
The judges' numbers come from a fixed scorer: height, rotation, clearance, sink rate at touchdown, wheel sync, distance from the mark and gas left in the tank. The commentary is written by an AI panel; the numbers never are.

JUDGED ON YOUR DEVICE
On iPhone and iPad with Apple Intelligence, the judges' commentary is written on the device by Apple's on-device model: no network, no cost, and nothing about your run is sent for it. The results say so, with the tokens it used. Without Apple Intelligence, an AI model on our own server writes it instead. Either way, the numbers come from the scorer, never the model.

COME BACK FOR
- A new daily jump with its own target, and a streak.
- Medals, and a board of the day's and all-time best landings, posted under three initials.
- Challenge a friend with a link to the same jump.

MADE FOR THE PHONE IN YOUR HAND
Drawn in Metal, up to 120 Hz on ProMotion iPhones. On iPhone Duo it fills the whole screen when open; in tabletop the top half is the screen and the bottom half the controller. Landings you feel through Core Haptics. Sound and haptics can each be turned down or off.

No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. An independent game: every car, course and sound is our own design.
