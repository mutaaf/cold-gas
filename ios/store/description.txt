One jump, made playable. A roadster climbs a kicker, fires ten cold-gas thrusters to clear a row of parked cars, rolls a full 360 in the air, retro-burns and lands on a pad. Three judges score it.

THE JUMP
The physics is a deterministic rigid-body sim at 240 ticks a second: raycast suspension, tyre grip, ten nozzles that run their tanks down, and a flight computer. The same inputs always fly the same jump, on the phone and on the web.

THREE WAYS TO FLY
- Assist: one thumb. Hold to climb, tap to let go, hold to land. The computer rolls you out and brakes.
- Pro: you fly the roll and the landing yourself.
- Advanced: all ten valves by hand.

THREE COURSES
The Show, the Wall and the Lineup, each with drills, a landing HUD and a coach that names what cost you points.

SCORED, NOT GUESSED
The judges' numbers come from a fixed scorer: amplitude, rotation, clearance, sink rate at touchdown, wheel sync, distance from the mark and gas left in the tanks. Every category is explained on the results. The commentary is written by an AI panel; the numbers never are.

COME BACK FOR
- A new daily jump with its own target.
- Medals, and a board of the day's and all-time best landings, posted under three initials.
- Challenge a friend with a link to the same jump.

MADE FOR THE PHONE IN YOUR HAND
Drawn in Metal at 120 Hz on ProMotion iPhones, laid out for every pose of iPhone Duo, with landings you feel through Core Haptics. Haptics, motion and controls can each be turned down or off.

No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. An independent game: every car, course and sound is our own design.
