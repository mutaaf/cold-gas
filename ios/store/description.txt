One stunt, made playable, and built for the screen in your hands. A stunt car climbs a curved wall on suction, lets go upside down, fires ten cold-gas thrusters to clear a row of parked cars and lands on the mark. Or it doesn't, and the pieces fly.

BUILT NATIVE FOR iPHONE AND iPAD
- The physics is a deterministic rigid-body sim at 240 ticks a second, written in Swift: raycast suspension, tyre grip, ten nozzles that run their tank down, and a flight computer. The same inputs always fly the same jump, on the phone and on the web.
- Drawn in Metal, up to 120 Hz on ProMotion displays.
- Felt through Core Haptics: each wheel touching down, every impact, a slide, the ABS. Haptics and sound can each be turned down or off.
- Sound made live on the device: the motor whining under load, tyres by surface, hits by material, the hiss of the jets.

iPHONE DUO, OPEN OR HALF OPEN
Open, the game runs edge to edge across the whole inner display, the controls over it. Half open in tabletop, the scene sits on the top screen and the controller on the bottom, like a handheld console. Closed, it's the phone layout. On iPad the controls sit at the side grips.

JUDGED ON YOUR DEVICE
Three judges score every jump. Their numbers come from a fixed scorer: height, rotation, clearance, sink rate at touchdown, wheel sync, distance from the mark and gas left in the tank. Their words come from an AI. On iPhone and iPad with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later), Apple's on-device model writes them: nothing about your run is sent for it, and the results show the tokens and the time it took. Without it, a model on our own server writes them; if neither answers, the game shows its own lines and says so. The numbers never come from a model.

FREE DRIVE AND ROAM
Roam the Show, the Wall and the Lineup between jumps, then drive on into a world that is built as you go: roads through forest and hills, cones and signs that move at their weight, fences and rails that break when hit hard. Free Drive starts out on the open road. Nothing is scored.

CHALLENGE A FRIEND
Share a picture of the jump, a landing or a wreck, with a link to beat it. With the app installed, the link opens it on your card: Take the challenge flies the same course and tier with your score to beat, then Send it back. Without the app, the link opens the web game.

THE JUMP
- Your first jump teaches itself: one tap to fly, one hint at each beat.
- Assist: one thumb. Hold to climb, tap to let go, hold to land.
- Pro: a stick and buttons. You fly the roll and the landing yourself.
- Advanced: all ten valves by hand.
- Move, resize and fade every touch control, with presets, for each way you hold the screen. A game controller or a keyboard works too.
- Three courses, drills for each part of the flight, and a coach that names what cost you points.

COME BACK FOR
- A new daily jump with its own target, and a streak.
- Medals, and a board of the day's and all-time best landings, posted under three initials.

No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. A few private usage counts, never sent during a run, with one switch in Settings to turn them off. An independent game: every car, course and sound is our own design.
