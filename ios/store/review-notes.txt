Thank you for reviewing Cold Gas. This 1.1.0 replaces the 1.0.0 we withdrew before review. No sign-in or demo account: there are no accounts. Everything is unlocked and the game plays offline.

A CORRECTION TO OUR 5 NOVEMBER REPLY (answer 3): our server does not fly posted runs again. It scores the run's telemetry (the numbers the app recorded) with the game's fixed scorer, after plausibility checks against the run's input log (course, timing, every number inside the car's limits); the tier is read from the log. The app never sends a score, but the telemetry comes from the app.

WHAT IT IS (4.3, 4.2.6)
An original physics skill game, written for iPhone and iPad in Swift and Metal (no template or app builder). A deterministic rigid-body sim at 240 ticks a second (tyres, suspension, ten cold-gas nozzles with a draining tank) flies a stunt car up a curved wall, over a row of parked cars and onto a mark. Depth: three courses (the Show, the Wall, the Lineup); three control tiers (Assist with one thumb, Pro with a stick, Advanced with all ten valves by hand); drills for each part of the flight; a coach that names what cost you points; a daily target with a streak; medals; Free Drive, an endless unscored world; three judges whose numbers come from the scorer and whose words come from Apple's on-device model where available. The same sim runs our web game, mutaafaziz.com/coldgas (public since October 2026).

HOW TO PLAY (about a minute)
1. Tap Fly The Show. A new player starts on Assist with one thumb.
2. Hold the round pad to climb; the car stops on the ledge. Tap once to let go.
3. Hold again to land: the computer rolls out and brakes onto the mark. Sliding the thumb moves the touchdown.
4. The results: score, the three judges and their words, Post, Fly again, Watch it (a replay), Share (a challenge link), Board, Home.
Title screen: the three courses, the Assist / Pro / Advanced tiers, Free Drive, and the icons top right: Practice (drills), Board, How to play, Settings. A game controller or a keyboard also works.

FREE DRIVE: tap Free Drive on the title. Stick to steer, GO and BRAKE, LIFT in the air. Back returns to the title. Nothing is scored.

THE JUDGES
The numbers always come from the fixed scorer. A model writes only the short commentary, from the run's numbers, never anything about the player.
- On a device with Apple Intelligence on (iOS 26+), Apple's on-device model writes it (Foundation Models). Nothing is sent. The results show "Judged on device" and a receipt: tokens, time, $0, no data left the device. Settings > Judges shows the model and can turn it off.
- Otherwise, or if it can't answer, a model on our own server (mutaafaziz.com) writes it from the run's numbers.
- Otherwise the app shows its own lines and says so.

THE BOARD
Posting is optional. The only thing typed is three letters (A to Z) as initials: no other text, profile or chat. Objectionable initials are filtered; touch and hold a row to hide or report it.

iPAD AND iPHONE DUO
Universal. On iPad the controls sit at the side grips. On iPhone Duo, open fills the inner display; tabletop puts the screen on top, the controller below.

NETWORK AND DATA
One host, our own: mutaafaziz.com. No third-party SDKs, ads or tracking, no advertising identifier, no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Sent when posting (run, course, tier, initials) and for server judging (the run). From 1.2.0, private usage counts (a jump started, ended or shared, with course, tier and score) go to our own analytics relay at mutaafaziz.com/boxoffice with a random install id made on the device, batched between runs. Settings > Privacy > Share usage stats turns them off and deletes the id. The label lists them (Product Interaction, Device ID; Analytics; not tracking).

RIGHTS
Everything is original: cars, courses, art, and sounds synthesised in code. No real brand, vehicle, logo or person. Fonts: Michroma, Geist (OFL 1.1). Physics: Rapier (Apache 2.0). No purchases, ads, gambling or loot boxes.
