Thank you for reviewing Cold Gas. This 1.1.0 replaces the 1.0.0 we withdrew before review. No sign-in and no demo account: there are no accounts. Everything is unlocked and the game plays offline.

A CORRECTION TO OUR 5 NOVEMBER REPLY (answer 3): our server does not fly posted runs again. When a run is posted, the server scores the run's telemetry (the flight's numbers the app recorded) with the same fixed scorer the game uses, after plausibility checks against the run's input log: the course, the timing, and every number inside the car's physical limits. The flight tier is read from the log itself. The app never sends a score, but the telemetry does come from the app. 1.1.0's text no longer says otherwise.

WHAT IT IS (4.3, 4.2.6)
An original physics skill game, written for iPhone and iPad in Swift and Metal (no template or app builder). A deterministic rigid-body sim at 240 ticks a second (tyres, suspension, ten cold-gas nozzles with a draining tank) flies a stunt car up a curved wall, over a row of parked cars and onto a mark. Depth: three courses (the Show, the Wall, the Lineup); three control tiers (Assist with one thumb, Pro with a stick, Advanced with all ten valves by hand); drills for each part of the flight; a coach that names what cost you points; a daily target with a streak; medals; Free Drive, an endless unscored world; three judges whose numbers come from the scorer and whose words come from Apple's on-device model where available. The same sim also runs our web game, mutaafaziz.com/coldgas (public since October 2026).

HOW TO PLAY (about a minute)
1. Tap Fly The Show. A new player starts on Assist with one thumb.
2. Hold the round pad to climb; the car stops on the ledge. Tap once to let go.
3. Hold again to land: the computer rolls out and brakes onto the mark. Sliding the thumb moves the touchdown.
4. The results: score, the three judges and their words, Post, Fly again, Watch it (a replay), Share (a challenge link), Board, Home.
Title screen: the three courses, the Assist / Pro / Advanced tiers, Free Drive, and the icons top right: Practice (drills), Board, How to play, Settings. A game controller or a keyboard also works.

FREE DRIVE: tap Free Drive on the title. Stick to steer, GO and BRAKE, LIFT in the air; the top-right buttons switch the camera, arm the fan and put the car back on the road. Back returns to the title. Nothing is scored.

THE JUDGES
The numbers always come from the fixed scorer. A model writes only the short commentary, from the run's numbers, never anything about the player.
- On a device with Apple Intelligence on (iOS 26+), Apple's on-device model writes it (Foundation Models). Nothing is sent. The results show "Judged on device" and a receipt: tokens, time, $0, no data left the device. Settings > Judges shows the model and can turn it off.
- Otherwise, or if it can't answer, a model on our own server (mutaafaziz.com) writes it from the run's numbers.
- If neither answers, the app shows its own lines and says so.

THE BOARD
Posting is optional. The only thing typed is three letters (A to Z) as initials: no other text, profile or chat. Objectionable initials are filtered in the app; touch and hold a row to hide it on the device or report it by email.

iPAD AND iPHONE DUO
Universal. On iPad the controls sit at the side grips, either orientation. On iPhone Duo, open fills the whole inner display; tabletop puts the screen on top and the controller below.

NETWORK AND DATA
One host, mutaafaziz.com/coldgas. No third-party SDKs, analytics, ads or tracking, no advertising identifier. Sent only when posting (run, course, tier, initials) and for server judging (the run).

RIGHTS
Everything is original: cars, courses, art, and sounds synthesised in code. No real brand, vehicle, logo or person. Fonts: Michroma and Geist (SIL OFL 1.1). Physics: Rapier (Apache 2.0). No purchases, ads, gambling or loot boxes; the same in every region.
