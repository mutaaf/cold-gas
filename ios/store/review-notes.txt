Thank you for reviewing Cold Gas 1.2.0. There are no accounts, so no sign-in or demo account. Everything is unlocked; it plays offline (the board and server judging need the network).

A STANDING CORRECTION TO OUR 5 NOVEMBER REPLY (answer 3): our server does not fly posted runs again. It scores the run's telemetry (the numbers the app recorded) with the game's fixed scorer, after plausibility checks against the run's input log (course, timing, every number inside the car's limits); the tier is read from the log. The app never sends a score, but the telemetry comes from the app.

WHAT IT IS (4.3, 4.2.6)
An original physics skill game written for iPhone and iPad in Swift and Metal (no template or app builder). A deterministic rigid-body sim at 240 ticks a second flies a stunt car over parked cars onto a mark; Core Haptics carries each touchdown and impact; iPhone Duo has its own layouts. The same sim runs our web game, mutaafaziz.com/coldgas.

HOW TO REACH EVERYTHING
1. A fresh install opens on the mark. Tap anywhere to fly (the Show, Assist, one thumb). One hint per beat: hold the round pad to climb, tap to let go, let it roll, hold to land. "I've got it" skips the hints.
2. Results after a landing: Next (next course), Beat <score>, Share, Post (optional), Watch it (replay), Roam, Board, Home. After a wreck: Fly again, Share.
3. Title: the three courses, the tiers (Assist, Pro, Advanced), Fly, Roam, Free Drive, and four icons top right: Practice (drills), Board, How to play, Settings.
4. Roam drives this course's set and on into the world; Free Drive starts on the open road. Stick to steer, GO, BRAKE; Pause (top) > Home returns. Neither is scored.
5. Settings > Controls > Edit the layout: move, resize and fade each control; presets, Undo, Reset.
6. iPad: controls at the side grips. iPhone Duo: open runs edge to edge; tabletop puts the screen on top, the controller below.
A game controller or a keyboard also works.

CHALLENGE LINKS
Share sends a picture and a link (mutaafaziz.com/coldgas/...#vs=...). With the app installed the link opens the app (universal link; coldgas:// fallback) on the sender's card: Take the challenge flies their course and tier against their score, then Send it back. Without the app it opens the web game. To try: Share a run to Notes, tap the link.

THE JUDGES
Numbers always come from the fixed scorer; a model writes only short commentary from the run's numbers.
- iOS 26+ with Apple Intelligence on: Apple's on-device model (Foundation Models) writes it. Nothing is sent. The results show "Judged on device" and a receipt: tokens, time, $0, no data left the device. Settings > Judges can turn it off.
- Without Apple Intelligence, with it off, or if it can't answer: a model on our own server (mutaafaziz.com) writes it.
- If neither answers in time, the app shows its own lines and says so.

USAGE COUNTS (NEW IN 1.2.0)
First-party, no SDK. A few counts (session, jump started/ended with course, tier, score, landed; first landing; share; challenge opened/played) and a random install id go to our own relay at mutaafaziz.com/boxoffice, which strips cookies, cuts the IP and forwards them to our analytics project (PostHog). Batched between runs, never during one. Settings > Privacy > Share usage stats turns them off and deletes the id. App Privacy: Product Interaction and Device ID, Analytics, linked, not tracking. No IDFA, no ATT prompt, no ads.

THE BOARD
Posting is optional: three letters (A to Z) as initials, no other text, profile or chat. Objectionable initials are filtered; touch and hold a row to hide or report it.

NETWORK
One host, our own: mutaafaziz.com. Sent: a post (run, course, tier, initials), server judging (the run), the counts above.

RIGHTS
Everything is original: cars, courses, art, and sounds synthesised in code. No real brand, vehicle, logo or person. Fonts: Michroma, Geist (OFL 1.1). Physics: Rapier (Apache 2.0). No purchases, ads, gambling or loot boxes.
