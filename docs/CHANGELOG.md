2026-10-27 · #0097 · Phones and tablets draw what a desktop draws (#136). An iPhone, an iPad or an Android phone or tablet now starts on the same graphics as a desktop: the night air with its haze and the halo around every lamp, the wet slab's reflection of the car and the Wall, the finer shadows, twice the cold-gas mist, Free Drive's distant forests, and the same sharpness (up to 2× the screen's pixels). The edges are smoothed by the filter that flickers least on a phone, which is the only other difference. If a device can't keep up, the game lowers its resolution first (in steps to 55 %), and only a device still struggling at the lowest resolution gets lighter graphics on its next visit. A phone that had been on the lighter graphics tries the full look on its next visit. The reflection's thin lights and girders flicker less on every screen. Phones show a little more shimmer in the slab's reflection and the far forests, as desktops already do; that's next (0098). No sim change.
