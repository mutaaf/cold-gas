## Defects, ranked by how badly they break the illusion

1. **Ad traffic runs inside the run (0117, P0, new).** The rule in §3.3 is "nothing in the countdown, a live run".
   - **What is clean:** the slots. No Marquee call fires in a run. With Jump pressed 0.8 s after the title, the 390×844
     run shows no ad element on screen 6 s in (`p390-ad-live.jpg`: only `.cue`, `.count` and `.touch` are visible).
   - **What isn't:** the title banner's AdSense pipeline. It finishes on its own clock:
     - **Jump at once:** 32 third-party requests, **427 KB (379 KB script)**, all finish during the *live run*. The ad
       request fires 7.4 s after the title.
     - **Jump 2 s in:** 21 requests (97 KB) and the ad request land in the countdown.
     - **`?play=jump`** (the launcher's link): 377 KB in the countdown and 62 KB in the run.
     - **1440×900:** 27–28 requests in the countdown for Jumps 0.5–3 s after the title, and 8–9 AdSense iframes inserted
       in the countdown.
   - **Only a 6 s wait on the title keeps it all on the title.** On a phone, ≈ 380 KB of script parsed during the run-up
     is a hitch in the one moment this game exists for. It also asks for an ad for a `display: none` slot.
   - **Cause:** `src/main.js:602-606` starts the loader and places the banner at idle whatever the mode.
     `tests/e2e/ads.spec.js` can't see this because it uses a stand-in loader.
2. **The car is a speck across the carry (0118, P1, new).**
   - **Production against the commit before #175:** the car's mean width / screen over the carry is **0.041** at 844×390
     (was 0.096), **0.061** at 1440×900 (was 0.129), and **0.049** at 1280×720 (was 0.116). They are identical on `9fdbcc4`.
   - **The frame never tightens.** At 844×390 the width holds at 0.040–0.043 from the row's front to its back, while the
     car falls from 20–25 m to 6–8 m.
   - **In real frames:** in `l844-5-over-row.jpg` (T+12.4, `06 BRAKE`) the 4.3 m roadster draws smaller than the gold
     coupe under it, and the top 55 % of the frame is sky and the launch complex. On the desktop
     (`d1440-4-release.jpg`) the car is ≈ 90 px wide in a 1440 frame.
   - **The cause:** 0113 traded the hero for the row. Both can hold: the span closes by more than half during the carry.
3. **Carried over, below a ticket's bar:**
   - The dead lower half under the upright touchdown. Unchanged; see `p390-7-down.jpg`.
   - The `0 mph` / `1 km/h` disagreement while the car settles (`src/units.js:66`).
   - 0028 (brake peak low and late) and 0027 / 0029 are still `proposed`. They are realism work, which waits out the
     lockdown.
