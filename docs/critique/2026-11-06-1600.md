  - At z −24.3: 3.7 m up, **FALL 12.7 ft/s at G 0.6 g**. Then the state box says **"HOLD · LAND" to a thumb that is

  - Sheet: Clean 83.7, sink 1.3 m/s, 6.1 m from the mark, "2.8 g braking peak at 6.7 m" (`repress-8-sheet.jpg`).
- **Why this is the reveal's bar.** The reveal's sink only falls from the onset: about 14 m/s at 12 m, about 1.5 m/s
  by 2.5 m, then a hover under 3 m (§2.1.4). This flight is different:
  - The car parks in the air 5.3 m over a truck.
  - It sinks down off it with the jets throttled to half a g.

  It's the flight 74 % of modelled phone players fly. The held thumb carries the brake past the semi in one burn
  (sink 8.0 → 5.7 → 1.9 m/s), so the same car on the same course can do it. The difference is what LAND does in
  the first second after the release.
- **0169's floor is fitted to the rule, not to the reveal.** It holds the jets at 0.51 g against a 0.50 g gate
  (measured minimum 0.501–0.509 g, the #271 PR). That's honest about what it is, and it no longer free-falls. But the
  descent off the semi is still a second descent, not the reveal's ease to a hover.

Filed as **0170** (P1, authenticity): the tapped thumb flies the held thumb's one-burn line past the semi.
