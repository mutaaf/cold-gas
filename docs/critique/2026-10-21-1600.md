## Defects, ranked by how badly they break the illusion

1. **On a portrait phone the telemetry covers the car (0026, P1).** The strip spans y 268–381 px at
   390×844. During the climb the car's projected centre is behind it in 31 of 80 frames, and over the
   row the hero car is drawn behind the G and N₂ cells (`phone-5-over-row.jpg`). It was noted at 09:45
   and left to 0018. 0018 shipped the right channels and didn't move them. At 844×390 and 1440×900
   it's 0 frames, so the fix is a portrait layout or a portrait camera, not a redesign.
2. **The car is symmetric, but the real one carries its tank in the passenger seat (0027, P1).** §11.4
   builds the CoM 6 cm toward the 400 L COPV, drifting to ≈ 3.4 cm as it empties. With the four
   down-jets at ±0.75 m, that's ≈ 4 kN·m of roll the flight computer has to trim on every LIFT. Trimming
   it costs lift, the way the pitch `TRIM` already does. Today the trim costs nothing because the
   offset doesn't exist. This is the next sourced spec value the sim hasn't taken. It's ESTIMATE, but
   computed from the OFFICIAL tank placement.
3. **Still open, and still the worst on the list:** the fan car corners at 1.36 g, not > 2 g (0020).
   The reference HUD says HANG over the row and ROLL 177° on the wheels (0022). The Wall reference
   still drives 21 s before the climb starts: the HUD probe saw the ramp begin at τ 21.25 s (0019). μ
   load sensitivity is still unsourced (0021). None of these shipped this cycle.
4. **G reads 1.4 with the car stopped** (`desk-7-touchdown.jpg`, `k1280-L2-touchdown.jpg`). That's the
   honest tail of a 0.5 s window that includes the stop. The livestream's 2 Hz channel would do the
   same, so it's noted, not ticketed.

## Combined slip: checked, not ticketed

At 09:45 I flagged the friction-circle clip (`vehicle.js:776`) as the next probe. I worked through
the pure-slip curves (`BX 11.5, CX 1.65, BY 16, CY 1.35`) against a typical Magic Formula combined-slip
weighting (Pacejka 2012 §4.3.2: G_yκ = cos(C_yκ·atan(B_yκ·κ)), B_yκ ≈ 6.5 at α 0.05, C_yκ ≈ 1.05):

- At the ABS slip peak (κ −0.1) with α 0.05 rad: the clip gives Fy 0.62·D, the MF weighting 0.65·D.
- At moderate braking (κ −0.03), same α: the clip gives 0.79·D, MF 0.77·D.

That's within 5 % where the car actually runs (ABS and TC hold κ near the peak in every tier). The
model is badly wrong only for a locked or spun wheel, where the force should follow the sliding
direction: 0.84·D sideways from the clip against ≈ 0.17·μ for a pure slide at 10°. ABS/TC prevent
that on the road. A wheel touching down without spin is 0017's case, so that's where it belongs.
