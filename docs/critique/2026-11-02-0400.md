   - **Sim:** one thumb, no slide, held to the ground, the touchdown is **`lateral ±5.31 m`**, **2.67–2.70 m past**,
     heading ±8.6°. That's 85.7 "Stuck it" at every tap delay.
   - **Never touched again after the tap:** `lateral ±6.11`, 2.6 m past, 80.7 Clean.
   - **The side flips with the tap's timing:** +5.31 at 0.25 and 0.35 s, −5.31 at 0.5 s.
   - **The coach's first finding is "Long of the mark" in all of them.**
   - **On production:** run (a) read *"Long of the mark. You landed 20 ft 6.2 m from the mark (10 ft 3.1 m past it):
     about 11 points"* with `lateral 5.39` (`2026-11-02-0400/11-results-8s.jpg`). Run (b) read *"…7 m from the mark
     (3.6 m past it): about 16 points"* with `lateral 5.94`.

   - **Why it costs the player:** about 80 % of the squared miss is across the run. A player who reads "long" and
     slides shorter fixes the small part and keeps the big one. Precision is the one category where one-thumb skill
     shows (hold 85.7 → the careful slide's median 93), so the coach has to name the real miss.

   - **0053:** first load. Usable at 7.9 s, the first 3D frame at 16 s on SwiftShader.
   - **0028:** braking still peaks at 3.0 g at 2.3 m, against 3.3 g at about 6.5 m.
