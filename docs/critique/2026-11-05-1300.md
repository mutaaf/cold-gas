## 1. On one thumb, a held sideways slide is a cliff, not a control (0164, P1)

Without a slide, the one-thumb Show lands 5.3 m left and 2.7 m past the mark: padMiss 6.0, Stuck it 85.7. The sight is
drawn from 23.0 m up, 4.0 s before touchdown, with the mark ≈ 36 px right. I slid sideways by X px, held it, and
re-flew the run for each X:

| held slide | lateral | padMiss | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | −5.3 m | 6.0 | Stuck it 85.7 |
| +8 / +10 | −4.8 / −3.9 | 5.5 / 4.6 | 88.0 / Perfect 10 92.0 |
| **+12** | **−9.3** | **9.8** | **Clean 74.7** |
| +14 / +16 / +18 | −6.9 / −5.2 / −3.4 | 7.4 / 5.7 / 4.1 | 79.0 / 87.0 / 93.7 |
| +20 / +24 / +28 | −1.7 / −2.9 / −2.6 | 2.7 / 3.3 / 3.8 | 95.3 / 96.0 / 94.7 |
| +32 | +6.0 | 7.1 | Clean 78.7 |
| **+36 … +48** | **+14.3 … +16.7** | **14.8 … 16.9** | **Rough landing 67–68**, 0.9–1.1 m over the roofs |
| **−12** | **−17.4** | **17.9** | **Rough landing 67.3** |
| −24 / −36 | −28.0 / −32.8 | 28.6 / 33.4 | Rough landing 66.0 / 65.7 |

- **The response isn't monotonic.** From 10 to 18 px, every 2 px swings the touchdown ±3 m, and half the swings go
  the wrong way.
- **The scale is far off.** `AIM_PX_PER_M = 6` (`src/onethumb.js:187`) promises 6 px a metre. The flight gives 0 to
  0.6 m a pixel, of either sign.
- **The sight's own position crashes the score.** Holding the "you" dot on the drawn mark (≈ 36–40 px of finger)
  turns a Stuck it into a Rough landing 16.7 m off.
- **A tiny slip does the same.** A 12 px slip the wrong way (about 2 mm on a phone) lands 17.9 m off.
- **Likely cause:** the roll is live from 23 m up, for about 3 s, and `flankAhead` switches it on and off tick by tick
  (`src/onethumb.js:155-168`).
- **The skill can still be reached.** A thumb that re-reads the dot 0.2–0.3 s late, at 100–1000 px/s, lands 2.0–3.2 m
  off (Perfect 10, 95.3–95.7). That's why the 03:54 production slide reached 95.7.
- **The skill isn't learnable, though.** On production today, the same single 40 px right / 10 px down slide flown at
  03:54 landed 7.2 m right: Clean 79.3, Daily missed (`slide-sheet-judges.jpg`). The same gesture gave 95.7 then and 79.3 now.
  The only difference is when in the 4 s window the finger moved. A real pilot's stick doesn't do that, and a player
  can't learn a control that does.

## 2. The sight's up/down half is a control that isn't there (0165, P1)

- **No effect at all.** A held slide straight up or down (±12, ±24, +36 px) leaves the touchdown exactly where it was:
  landZ −32.7, padMiss 5.9–6.0, 85.7. The pitch was non-zero for 0.04 s of the whole flight.
- **Why:** `rowBehind` (`src/onethumb.js:71`) opens pitch only past z −20.1. The traced flight gets there at h 3.3 m
  and reaches h 2.5 m (where `aimFade` starts closing) by z −21.8.
- **But the sight still draws it.** The dot shows the 2.7 m along-track miss, and the coach says "slide onto the amber
  dot".
- **It sets the floor for every careful thumb.** That 2.7 m is why the tracking thumbs above can't get under 2 m.
