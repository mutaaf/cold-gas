## Defects, ranked by how badly they break the illusion

1. **The landing brake sits in the last 3 m, not at 6.5 m (0028, P1).** The reveal's one famous
   landing number is a height: 3.3 G "at about 6.5 metres". Run 2 started braking at 12 m (1.7 G),
   peaked at 6.5 m, eased to 0.3 G at 2.3 m and flared to 1.4 G at 1.2 m. It touched down 2.5 s after
   the peak. Ours free-falls to 14 m at 14.2 m/s, starts braking near 7 m, and peaks at 3.3 m (Wall)
   or 2.3 m (Show). On production the car crosses 5.4 m at 13.4 m/s sink and 1.2 G
   (`desk-5-over-row.jpg`); on the phone it's 6.2 m at 11.8 m/s (`phone-5-over-row.jpg`). Then the
   card prints "3 g braking peak at 2.3 m" (`desk-8-results.jpg`). The value is right but the height
   is a third to a half of the reveal's. The 03:45 critique deferred this until 0014's set-down law
   landed. It has. 0013's gate ("below 10 m") is too loose to catch it.
2. **The row is parked backwards (0029, P1).** In the side shot the gold cab comes first and the dark
   sedan last. Ours is the reverse, the crossover is the dark car, and the row is 12.6 m against
   ≈ 15–17 m. The reason VEHICLES.md gives for keeping it ("so the Show's geometry stays put") expired
   when 0014 and 0019 re-tuned the Show. Focus item 4 asks for exactly this.
3. **The HUD still lies in two places (0022, open, sharpened).** Over the row the timeline reads
   "03 HANG" and ROLL reads 187–191° with the car upright (`desk-5-over-row.jpg`). At touchdown ROLL
   reads 177°. In the crash the car lies on its side and ROLL reads **2°**
   (`phone-9b-crash-after.jpg`). I added that case to 0022's criteria.
4. **On a portrait phone the strip still covers the car (0026, open).** The strip is unchanged at
   y 268–381 px. Over the row the hero car is drawn behind the N₂ and G cells (`phone-5-over-row.jpg`).
5. **Fan-armed corners understeer hard.** This is noted, not ticketed: no source for the real car's
   balance. At 20 m/s with full stick the fronts run at 11.4° of slip against the rears' 4.4°. The g
   still rises with stick (0.8 → 1.0: 2.14 → 2.40 g), so the front axle isn't past its peak. 0021's
   builder flagged that the rack and ESC plan with a ≈ 3.6 g budget where the fitted tyres give
   ≈ 3.0 g. Ticket it if a player probe shows full stick losing g.
