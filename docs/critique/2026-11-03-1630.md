1. **A thumb off the pad in the roll-out throws the car long (0140, P1, new).** In Node, holding from the tap and
   lifting the thumb N s later, never pressing again:

   - **0.9 s:** 11.8 m, 5.2 m over, Rough landing 70.7.
   - **1.0 s:** 16.1 m, 10.2 m over, Rough landing 67.3.

   - **1.5 s:** 7.5 m, Clean 78.3.
   - **Held to the ground:** 6.0 m, 4.5 m over, Stuck it 85.7.

   Pressing again 0.25–2 s after a lift at 1.1 s changes nothing: the car still lands 15–16 m off. The touchdown
   sink is 1.0 m/s in every one of these flights. The car isn't dropped, it's thrown down the course.
   - **The cause in the code:** `out.level` is only set while the thumb is down (`src/onethumb.js`, `sample`, the
     `fly` stage). A thumb that leaves mid-roll leaves the lift vector tilted.
   - **What the real car did:** rolled upright in about 1.1 s and was "helped … gently land" on its pad
     (`docs/REVEAL_RESEARCH.md`, the run-2 timeline).
   - **What Assist promises on the sheet:** "rolls you out, levels and brakes" (`phone-held-results.jpg`).
   - **The bar:** wherever the thumb comes off and goes back on, the touchdown stays within 8 m of the mark, and
     a quarter second of thumb timing moves it ≤ 3 m.
