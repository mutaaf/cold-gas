## Defects, ranked by how badly they break the illusion

1. **The fan is a switch, not a controller (0012, P0).** The reveal's DOWNFORCE channel is the
   most-quoted number of the night and it climbs with pitch. Ours reads 4,689 kgf on the flat at
   the foot of the curl (`src/sim/vehicle.js:364-367`: force = command × 46 kN). The tyres carry
   2.9 g of extra load up the first quarter of the arc, and the HUD's FAN readout is a 0–100 %
   command, not kgf (`desk-3-inverted.jpg`: "FAN 100 %" on the vertical). Bar: suction within
   ±30 % of the telemetry at six pitches, ≤ 250 kgf parked, 1–3 s spool.
2. **The car is shoved off the lip, and the brake is a long 2 G burn (0013, P0).** Inverted,
   LAND's roll-out opens down-jets that point at the ground: 2.4–2.9 G in the first 0.25 s, when
   the livestream reads 0.2. Then the stop is spread over 40 ft at ≈2 G instead of peaking at
   3.3 G near 6.5 m. The results chip then prints "3.5 g peak" (`desk-9-results.jpg`), which is
   the release kick, so the one number a player would compare with the reveal is the wrong one.
3. **The kickflip hovers for 2.3 s before it lands (0014, P1).** The Lineup reference is right to
   the apex and through the roll, then drifts 15 m at 1 m altitude at 0.55 m/s sink. Airtime
   5.5 s against 3.0, distance 40.6 m against 20–25, and 84 kg of gas spent going nowhere.
4. **On-screen telemetry is not the livestream's** (no ticket this run; cap is 3). The reveal put
   DOWNFORCE (kg), PITCH (°), ALTITUDE (ft) and G-FORCE on screen. The HUD shows Air %, Roll
   (accumulated: 190° at 14 m in the Show's fall, `desk-5-over-row.jpg`), Alt, Sink, Load and
   Fan %. There is no tank or line pressure anywhere in flight, though the regulator
   (`regulatedFraction` 0.55) is exactly what decides whether the last metre of a stop works.
   Next run: a ticket for a livestream-honest strip (kgf, pitch, bar, regulated/blowdown).
5. **Phone portrait: the HUD sits on the car** (`phone-5-over-row.jpg`). At 390×844 the Air
   gauge and the attitude indicator overlap the car body while it falls past 13.5 m, and the
   ground takes half the frame. Mobile playability is a keep, so this belongs to the Scout's
   queue; noted here as evidence.
6. **The Wall course starts 120 m from the curl.** The reference drives 20 s at 6 m/s before it
   reaches the ramp; the reveal rolled from rest at the foot. It's dead time, not a physics
   defect, and the Show already starts close.

## What is now right (with evidence)

- **The curl and the hang.** Climb at 4.7 m/s on suction, a 170° deck, stationary inverted hang
  (`desk-3-inverted.jpg`), the 180° roll-out off the lip in about the reveal's time.
- **One fill is one stunt.** 69 % of the 297 kg fill on the Wall, and the regulator falls off
  (feed pressure 0.94 at 14 ft, 0.53 at touchdown), so a late stop has to be planned.
- **The kickflip's flight to the apex**: 7.6 m, 360°, inverted over the row at t0 + 1.1 s.
- **0–60 in 0.61 s** with all systems, against the site's < 0.59 s.
- **Soft, four-wheel touchdowns** at 0.7–1.0 m/s, wheel spread 25–71 ms, no bottoming
  (max compression 0.06–0.09 m of 0.16 m travel).
- **Accessibility gates hold:** 32/32 naive Wall, 54/54 naive Show (51 over the row), 91/91 unit.
- **Own origin only**: one host in the production request log, zero console errors.

## Note to the operator

- **The 27.7 m lip.** #19 raised the lip from the sourced 20 m (16–21 m) to 27.7 m "by design".
  With `focus` set to REALISM, this is the largest single departure from the research in the
  game: every release-height, fall-speed and braking number above starts 7.7 m too high, and a
  reveal-literate player will see it in the first climb. I have not ticketed it because it was
  your call; if you want the reveal's Wall back, say so and it becomes a P0.
- **Tyres at the limit** (focus item 1): there is no relaxation length (forces are instant at
  240 Hz) and the combined slip is a friction-circle clamp on pure-slip Pacejka. Neither is
  visible in the reference runs; they need a dedicated probe (a step steer and a combined
  brake-and-turn at 2.0 m tyre load) before they deserve a ticket.
