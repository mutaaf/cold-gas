## Defects, ranked by how badly they break the illusion

1. **An upright phone sees three of the five vehicles the car clears (0116, P1, filed).**
   - **The frame:** `p390-A-4-over-row.jpg` (T+12.5) shows half the Semi, the Pickup and the Roadster. The
     two bodies behind the Semi are off the left edge, and the bottom 30 % of the frame is bare concrete.
   - **Measured:** the row's mean on-screen share through the crossing is **60–61 %** at 360×800, 390×844
     and 412×915. **15–17 of 52** frames have less than half the row in frame, and 12–14 have fewer than 3
     bodies.
   - **Control:** 844×390 and 1440×900 read **92 %**, with 4 bad frames, all from the locked-off hold.
   - **Why the probes miss it:** 0113 recorded 390×844 as 0 % *covered*, which is true, because the HUD is at
     the top. Nothing measured the row going off the *edges*.
   - **Cause:** the carry's fixed 54° vertical FOV at 21 m (`src/render/director.js:229-232`). At aspect 0.46
     that shows ≈ 9.9 m of ground against the row's 12.6 m. This is the game's money shot on the device most
     players hold upright.
2. **On a landscape phone the row slides under the HUD (0113, P1, ready).** Unchanged: the director wasn't
   touched. The same 30 fps stepping shows the row 100 % *on screen* at mid-row at 844×390, so the remaining
   loss there is all HUD cover, which is 0113's measure.
3. **On an upright phone the touchdown is wider than the screen (0108, P1, ready).**
   - `p390-A-6-touchdown.jpg`: no nose, the parked Roadster's tail clipping in at the left, and the bottom
     40 % black pad.
   - `p390-C-6-touchdown.jpg`: the same for the wreck, plus a jet tube against the sky.
   - Unchanged since 04:11.
4. **The rest of the open critic queue is unchanged and was not re-measured:** 0069 (sharpened 04:11), 0083
