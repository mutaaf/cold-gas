## Defects, ranked by how badly they break the illusion

1. **The gas Daily turns on one choice nobody is told about, and aiming is punished for it (0139, P1, new).**
   - **The choice:** held from the tap, both production flights landed with **34 %** gas (`phone-held-touchdown.jpg`:
     GAS 222 lb; the card's `airPct` 34, 34). The builder measured 33–37 % held from the tap and 23–26 % when
     the pad is picked up once the fall has begun (0134 Outcome). The bar is > 30 %.
   - **The population:** `human-phone 150 --scheme=thumb --daily`:
     - `--skill=none`: gas-show **39/150 (26 %)**, gas min/median/max 26/26/33 %, median score 83.7.
     - `--skill=careful`: gas-show **28/150 (19 %)**, gas 23/25/37 %, median score **92.7**.
     - So the players who fly better (9 points better) meet the gas Daily *less* often. Sliding onto the mark
       costs lift, and that is physically honest: the real pilot's steering onto the pad cost gas too. What is
       not honest is a Daily that is told as "Clear the row with more than 30 % gas left" when the only lever is
       when you put your thumb down.
   - **What the player sees on a miss:** `Daily: 28 % gas left, need 31 % — try again` (`src/comeback.js:213`,
     the miss text from `src/daily.js:32`). Nothing names the pad, the tap or the slide. The coach mentions gas
     only below 10 % (`src/sim/coach.js:233`). The builder flagged it as a known trade-off in 0134's Outcome.
   - **When:** gas-show is the Daily on **2026-11-15** and **2026-11-20** (`objectiveFor`).
   - **The bar:** a phone player who misses the gas Daily is told, in the device's words, the one thing that
     separates 34 % from 25 %, and a careful population told it meets it at least as often as a no-skill one.
2. **0135's floor is now below its own line for gas-show (sharpened, not new).** 0135 asks ≥ 30 % reach per
   objective "gas-show included once 0134 ships". 0134 shipped at 19 % careful / 26 % none. A lower bar alone
   (> 25 %) would hand it to 150/150 no-skill players, a free day. 0139's words are the better lever; 0135's
   table should be re-measured after 0139. A dated note is added to 0135.
