## Defects, ranked by how badly they break the illusion

1. **On a phone the telemetry isn't on the screen (0018, P0).** At 390×844 touch, `.hud-right`
   spans x = 371–522 px. ALT, SINK, LOAD and FAN render at x = 470–494 px, past the 390 px edge,
   and the attitude dial is a sliver at the right edge (`phone-hud-runup.jpg`). On desktop the
   strip shows kgf (0012 shipped that). But there is **no pitch** (the livestream's "170°"), and
   ROLL reads 0° while the car hangs upside down (`desk-3-inverted.jpg`). The gas gauge says
   "AIR 100 %" under a title that says nitrogen at 690 bar, with no tank pressure and no sign of
   when the regulator lets go. LOAD is a per-frame filter (`src/main.js:313`), not the chip's
   0.5 s window. At touchdown the pad's white ring runs through SINK and LOAD with no backing
   (`desk-7-landing.jpg`, invariant 3.8).
2. **The kickflip still hovers after it is upright (0014, re-scoped to `ready`).** On the Lineup
   reference, the car is upright at 7.3 m at τ 1.75 s and doesn't touch until τ 4.80 s. It spends
   2.0 s of that at about 1 G between 2.3 m and the ground. The reveal went from upright to wheels
   in about 0.8 s after both low stunts. The mark sits 22 m past the row, so the precision score
   pays for the drift. The builder showed that one fixed set-down law can't serve both the Wall and
   the Lineup. The reveal's own data says the set-down depends on the fall it ends, so the
   re-scope asks for exactly that, keeps the Wall on its telemetry (`gtrace.test.js`), and moves
   the mark to just past the row.
3. **The tanks fly isothermal (0016 sharpened).** Integrating `vehicle.js:797-812` for a full dump
   gives 186 kN·s at a 64 s average Isp. The research's real-gas blowdown gives 140 kN·s at 52 s
   for the realistic adiabatic dump. The regulator (0.55) keeps full thrust down to 45 % gas, so
   thrust stays above half for 1.83 s of continuous firing, against 0.65 s adiabatic. The gas
   bill for every stunt is about 25 % cheap. 0016 (scout) is the right ticket, but its criterion
   "end-of-brake thrust within 5 % of today's" would keep the error, and its closed form is off
   (0.6^0.4 = 0.815, not 0.88). I added a Critic note with an impulse bar.
4. **The Wall starts 120 m from the curl (0019, P1).** The reference drives 20 s at 6 m/s before
   the fan arms. The reveal's car sat parked at the foot, spooled up at rest, and was at 25° about
   3 s later. The spool that 0012 fitted to the telemetry never happens on camera.
5. **The braking peak is low** (no ticket; 0013's bar is "below 10 m" and it passes). The peak is
   3.1 G at 3.3 m (Wall) and 3.0 G at 2.3 m (Show), against 3.3 G at 6.5 m. The reveal then dipped
   to 0.3 G at 2.3 m and flared at 1.4 G at 1.2 m. Ours goes from brake straight into a 1 G
   set-down, with no dip and no flare. Worth a ticket once 0014's fall-dependent set-down lands,
   since that is the same law.
6. **Title, cosmetic** (Scout's queue, not ticketed under the realism focus). The top-bar wordmark
   "COLD GAS" is drawn over the "TEST STAND · NIGHT" kicker at 1440×900 (`desk-0-title.jpg`).

## What is now right (with evidence)

- **The fan is a controller.** It reads 127 kgf rolling on the pad, rises with tilt to about 2,440
  kgf on the vertical (where the car nearly stops at 0.3 m/s, like run 1), reaches 4,668 kgf
  inverted, and the HUD shows it in kgf (`desk-3-inverted.jpg`: "FAN 4092 KGF"). Tyre load on the
  curl is 3,060–3,430 kgf; the old switch put 2.9 g on the tyres from the first metre.
- **The release falls free and the brake is late and hard.** It reads 0.36–0.50 G over the first
  second, a 3.1 G peak, wheels 4.77 s after release, and 1.9 s below 3 m. The results chip says
  "3 g braking peak at 2.3 m" (`desk-9-results.jpg`), not the release tick.
- **The landing physics holds up in a drop test.** A 2.4 m/s contact peaks at 3.9 g on the tyres
  with 0.10 m compression, which matches the energy balance (½v² over 0.07 m is 4.3 g). The
  bump stop isn't reached until about 5.5 m/s, where the hull touches and damage starts (8 %).
  At 8.3 m/s it is 14 g and 38 % damage, and rebound stays at e ≈ 0.2–0.25. The references touch
  at 0.8 m/s with 0.04–0.09 m compression and 1.5–1.8 g of tyre load.
- **Accessibility and determinism gates hold.** 32/32, 54/54, 187/187 unit tests, and cue
  followers 95/100. That is exactly the bar, so any further change to the governor has no slack.
- **Own origin only**, with zero console errors on desktop and phone.
