## 2. Defects, ranked
1. **0374 (P1, new): a careful thumb held from the tap can't reach the 2 m bullseye.** It lands 2 m long.
   - **The pad teaches the hold.** On production the pad reads "Tap · Let go", then "Hold · Land" 0.3 s later
     (`phone-first.log.txt`). A thumb that stays down from the tap is obeying the pad.
   - **Over 300 careful thumbs** (`ring-probe.summary.txt`):
     - All **52** careful runs that land outside the ring are thumbs held from the tap. That's 52 of 78.
     - Every one of those 78 lands **1.64–2.19 m past the mark**.
     - Sideways they're near-perfect: median 0.08 m.
     - **0 of 222** thumbs that lifted and pressed again miss the ring.
   - **Why it happens:**
     - At the last sight read with the car ≥ 2.5 m up, the along-run slide is still 0 on **57 of 78** held runs.
     - `canA` (`src/onethumb.js:351`) waits for `braked`.
     - `braked` needs the sink to have peaked above 4 m/s (`:348`).
     - A thumb holding LAND from the release probably never sinks that fast, so the sight draws no along-run miss
       (`markOffset`, `:475`). The car then lands 2 m long under a dot that says nothing is wrong.
   - **The bar:** the skill the card pays for has to be reachable by the hold the pad teaches. 0371 now tells these
     players "Land inside the 2 m ring for full marks: this one was 2.2 m", and their controls can't do it.
