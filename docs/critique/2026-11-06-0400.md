## 1. The one-thumb Show's brake goes out at 6.5 m (0169, P0)

The reveal's burn is one pulse: 1.7 G at 12 m, **3.3 G at about 6.5 m**, 1.0 G at 3.1 m, then a 1.4 G flare at
1.2 m (REVEAL_RESEARCH §2.1.4). Here is the one-thumb Show, held from the tap, which is also what the production phone
run flew (Stuck it 86.0):

| after release | height | sink | specific force |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.70–2.20 s | 11.9 → 6.8 m | 14.5 → 6.1 m/s | 2.7–3.0 g |
| 2.25 s | 6.56 m | 5.75 m/s | 0.63 g |
| **2.30–2.45 s** | **6.3 → 5.2 m** | **6.1 → 7.6 m/s** | **0.00–0.11 g** |
| 2.50 s | 4.85 m | 8.0 m/s | 0.51 g |
| 2.55–2.85 s | 4.4 → 3.0 m | 7.5 → 2.3 m/s | 2.4–3.0 g |

- **Cause.** In the tick the jets go out, LAND's altimeter (`landHeight`, `src/sim/vehicle.js:482`) reads **2.29 m,
  then 6.11 m**. The car is at z −18.16, x −4.58, and its footprint has just left the last roof under its track.
  The wanted sink (`landWantAt`, `vehicle.js:468`) jumps from 5.67 to **10.73 m/s**. The governor finds the car
  "ahead" of the new profile and stops lifting (`vehicle.js:451`).
- **Not a one-off.** Held sideways slides of 0, +8 … +32 px each open a 0.14–0.32 s gap at 5.4–7.0 m. Slides of −12
  and −24 px open a 0.15–0.17 s gap at 7.0–7.5 m. Only +40/+48 px (15–17 m wide) brake in one pulse.
- **The Lineup reference does it too.** At the end of its row (z 22.8) it's off for 0.21 s at 3.1 m, sink 5.0 → 6.8 m/s.
- **The Wall has no roofs and brakes in one pulse,** on one thumb at every slide and in the reference. So do the Show
  reference and the Wall reference.
- **The 11-05 critique missed this.** It called this brake "close to the reveal" from the peak alone (2.76 g at
  8.7 m). A 0.25 s mean of the same trace reads 1.25 g and 1.15 g across the gap, so it hides (LESSONS row added).
- **The bar:** one continuous burn from the onset to the wheels. Sink never rises again once the brake has slowed it.
  The plumes never go out over the row.
