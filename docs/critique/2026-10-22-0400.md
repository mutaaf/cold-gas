## Defects, ranked by how badly they break the illusion

1. **The release happens behind the HUD (0063, P1).** For the first ≈ 0.9 s after the car lets go of
   the ledge, its centre sits at y 62–91 px:
   - at 1440×900, behind the timeline (bottom 129 px) in **10 of 41 air frames**;
   - at 390×844, behind Retry and the timeline (bottom 112 px) in **9 of 41**.

   `desk-4-release.jpg` shows the car under "03 HANG · 04 RELEASE" with its roof cut by the frame
   edge, while the lower two-thirds of the frame is empty concrete. `phone-4-release.jpg` and
   `phone-C1-rolling.jpg` show it half off the top. The cause is the Show carry rig: it aims at 55 % of
   the car's height from a camera capped at 10 m (`src/render/director.js:155-159`).
2. **The timeline says BRAKE through a free fall (0065, P1).** After the release, CARRY shows for
   0.2 s. Then the roll-out's attitude pulses push the lift-nozzle sum over 1.2 (`src/main.js:495`),
   and the monotonic timeline locks onto 06 BRAKE from 23.7 m. At 1.25–1.5 s after release the car is
   falling at **0.03 g** under a lit BRAKE. On screen that's "06 BRAKE" above G 0.5 at 19.8 m
   (`desk-4-release.jpg`). The real car carried for 1.5 s and started braking at 12 m.
3. **Every judged run throws WebGL errors (0064, P1).** Reproduced on production at 390×844 with one
   reference run and nothing else: at the results, with the theatre on its compute stage, it logged
   `useProgram: attempt to use a deleted object`, `uniform2f/uniform1f: location is not from the
   associated program`, 4× `getUniformLocation: attempt to use a deleted object` and `bindBuffer:
   attempt to use a deleted object`. The 1440×900 play-through logged the same set. These are raw-GL
   calls (aurora-field, not three) on a canvas the theatre reuses for every call
   (`src/ai/theatre.js:149/197/417`). Nothing visibly broke here, but it's a draw with freed GL objects
   in the one screen that shows the AI working.
4. **Over the row, the car is still drawn behind the strip on a portrait phone (0026, open).**
   `phone-5-over-row.jpg`: the hero is under the N₂ and G cells at 4.3 m. This is unchanged since
   22:00.
5. **The landing brake sits low and the row is backwards (0028, 0029, open).** Both are unchanged
   since 22:00, and both wait for the lockdown.
6. **Noted, not ticketed.**
   - On a crashed car lying on its side, two plumes kept firing (`desk-C2-down.jpg`, "N₂ BLOWDOWN").
     That's my held roll stick, and #73 (0062, open) shuts the valves after a crash.
   - On a phone the theatre covers the results full-screen, so Replay can't be tapped for the 30–40 s
     the call took here. That's by design (there's a Skip), but a slow edge model makes it long.
