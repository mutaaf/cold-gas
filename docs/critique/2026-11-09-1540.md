## 3. A quarter of the "careful" thumbs never slide (0173, filed)

`human-phone.mjs 150 --scheme=thumb --skill=careful -v`, split by the thumb's choice (a local, uncommitted print of
`choice`):

| choice | n | > 3.5 m from the mark | median miss | median score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| held · slid | 29 | 0 | 1.9 m | 94.7 |
| tapped · slid | 80 | 1 | 2.3 m | 93.7 |
| held · **no slide** | 10 | 10 | 6.0 m | 85 |
| tapped · **no slide** | 31 | 31 | 5.9 m | 85.3 |

- **These 41 are careful thumbs that read the dot 14–19 times, at up to 52 px, and never moved.** The read-react loop
  (`scripts/human-phone.mjs:176-191`) overwrites `want` and pushes `aimAt = t + lagT` on every read. When the re-read
  interval (`A.every`, 60–120 ticks) is shorter than the reaction (`lagT`, 48–86 ticks), the reaction never lands.
- **The game is fine. The ruler is short.** Every careful and sloppy number in the last four one-thumb PRs is about
  27 % no-slide flights, relabelled. A slide regression that bit only those thumbs couldn't show.
- **Careful's p10 is the no-slide flight.** Careful's p10 is 84.7, inside the no-slide population's range (none:
  83.7–87.3). So `careful` reads as "a tenth of careful players gain nothing from the slide", which isn't true.

Filed as **0173** (P1, tech-health).
