## Defects, ranked by how badly they break the illusion

1. **The car crosses the row under the held pad (0128, P0, in PR #204).** Unchanged on production.
2. **The tap on the ledge goes unanswered for 0.92 s (0129, P1, new).**
   - **(b) Real touches:** after the tap's `touchend`, `[data-thumb-act]` read **"Let go"** at sim **+0.1, +0.2,
     +0.3 and +0.4 s** with `S.released = false`. The next sample was +1.3 s, already "Land" and airborne.
   - **(a) Stepped:** the label flips from "Let go" to "Land" at exactly **+0.92 s**, the frame `S.released` goes
     true, while the car is still on the deck (`ramp`). It's airborne at +1.09 s.
   - **Why:** the tap only sets the drive-off (`src/onethumb.js:117`). The push-off waits for `THUMB.lip` 0.98
     (`:119`), and the pad's words key off `S.released` (`thumbStage`, `src/onethumb.js:87`; `noteRelease`,
     `src/main.js:765`).
   - **No other answer:** the only one is `buzz('tap')` (`src/input.js:189`), and iPhones have no vibration API.
   - **The bar:** a response under about 0.1 s reads as immediate (Miller 1968; Card, Moran & Newell). The drive-off
     itself is honest: the real launch spent "under 1 s at the lip" (REVEAL_RESEARCH §2.1.4). A second of "Tap · Let
     go" after the tap is not.
3. **Where the newcomer's long hang goes (last critique's defect 4, now split).** In the sim, `invertedTime`:

   | flight | total | climb | crawl ≥ 0.85 | cue → tap | tap → lip (drive-off) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | reference (`createAutopilot`) | **3.65 s** | 2.76 | 0.89 | — | — |
   | one thumb, tap +0.25 s | **5.07 s** | 2.67 | 1.16 | 0.25 | **0.99** |
   | one thumb, tap +0.5 s | 5.32 s | 2.67 | 1.16 | 0.50 | 0.99 |
   | one thumb, tap +1.0 s | 5.82 s | 2.67 | 1.16 | 1.00 | 0.99 |

   - **What it adds up to:** about **1.26 s** of the one-thumb excess is the machine's (+0.27 s of slower crawl to
     the stop, +0.99 s of drive-off from a standstill). The rest is the player's reaction to the cue. A first-visit
     reaction of about 0.7–0.9 s gives the 6.0 s measured at 16:00.
   - **Verdict:** a stop-then-tap hang is run 1's behaviour (it hung about 10 s, §2.1.2), so the hang length is not
     fake. The defect is the silent second after the tap, which is 0129. Shortening the drive-off would be a sim
     change, so it's left out of 0129's scope on purpose.
