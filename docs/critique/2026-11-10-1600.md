## 2. On one thumb, "Hold · Land" mostly changes nothing (0188, filed)

The production first visit gave this away:
- A thumb that held LAND as the pad said scored **81.0** (Clean, 6.7 m off, `r1-results.png`).
- A thumb that let go at the ledge and never touched the pad again scored **82.7** (Clean, 6.5 m off,
  `c-results.png`).

In Node, with no slide: tap on the ledge, lift 20 ticks later, press again N s after the tap. "=" marks an end
state identical to the bit to never pressing again:

| course | never pressed | 0.3–0.7 s | 0.75–0.85 s | 0.9–1.1 s | 1.2–2.5 s, every 0.1 s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Show | 83.7, 6.2 m | 84.7 | **78.0–79.0**, 6.9 m | 85.7–86.7 | **83.7 =** |
| Wall | 90.7, 11 m | 90.7 | 90.3 | 90.3–90.7 | **90.7 =** |

- **Assist's auto-land engages 1.17 s after the tap on the Show (1.19 s on the Wall).** It flies "exactly what LAND
  held from the release would have flown" (`src/sim/vehicle.js:579-603`), so every later press is the same flight.
  That's by design, and it's why 32/32 naive players land.
- **The pad doesn't say any of this.** Timed per frame on production, with the thumb off after the tap
  (`c-warn.png`):

  | run time | what happens |
  |---|---|
  | 12.80 s | the pad pulses amber, "Hold · Land" |
  | 13.16 s | auto-land engages |
  | 14.23 s | the pulse stops |

  For 1.07 s, the pulse asks for a press that can no longer change anything.
- **The hold's real job is carrying the slide,** worth about 10 points (careful slides, median 93.3–94.7, against
  83.7). The pad never names it in the air. Only the results' coach does.

The bar: the pad's words and its amber name what a press does at that moment, or the press does what the words say.
