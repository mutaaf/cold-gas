## What is now right (with evidence)

- **The pause sheet holds the keyboard (0054, #134).**
  - Escape pauses, and focus lands on the dialog box with no ring (`DIV[Paused]`, `outline-style: none`).
  - Tab walks Resume → Restart → Controls → How to fly → Home and wraps to Resume. Shift+Tab goes back.
    Focus never left the sheet in 8 presses, and each focused button shows a ring
    (`desk-paused-tab.jpg`: Restart ringed).
  - Escape resumes, and focus drops to `BODY`, so the next key belongs to the car.
- **Controls over the pause sheet are stacked right.** Enter on Controls opens it with focus inside.
  Escape closes only Controls, and focus comes back to the Controls button with the game still paused.
- **A keyboard resume flies the car.** Enter on Resume put focus back on the opener (the top bar's Home,
  which Tab had reached during the run). Then ↑ with Space moved the car from 0.0009 to 2.27 m/s in
  192 ticks. The run stayed `live` and unpaused: Space on the focused Home did not click it (src/input.js
  guards it).
- **The phone gets no stray ring.** After the background pause, focus is on the dialog with no outline.
  A tap on Resume resumes and leaves focus on `BODY`.
- **A pause mid-air costs the sim nothing.** 94.0 and 0.817 m/s, identical to the unpaused reference.
