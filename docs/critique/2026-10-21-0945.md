## Defects, ranked by how badly they break the illusion

1. **The fan car doesn't corner like a fan car (0020, P1).** The stage's one cornering claim is
   "greater than 2 G's" with the fans (OFFICIAL). With `fan: 1` on the level at 30 m/s and full
   stick, the game holds 1.19 g and the fan idles at 133 kgf. Two causes. First, the fan schedule
   (`vehicle.js:613-628`) has only a tilt term and a straight-line "void" term, so a corner never
   asks for suction. Second, the rack's full lock is `L·μg/v² + 0.04` (`vehicle.js:600`), sized
   for 1.25 g, so even with 4.6 t of suction forced on, the front tyres run at 1.6–4.3° slip and
   the corner tops out at 1.8 g at 20 m/s. Tyres alone hold 1.12–1.14 g steady against the OFFICIAL
   1.26 g, and the same rack cap is why. A reveal engineer would call this out first in Free Drive.
2. **The reference flight's HUD lies (0022, P1, S).** "Watch the reveal" shows **03 HANG** at
   T+12.5 s, 6.2 m up, sinking at 11.7 m/s over the row (`desk-5-over-row.jpg`,
   `phone-5-over-row.jpg`, `phone-6-rollout.jpg`). `S.released` is set only in `wallAssist`
   (`main.js:307`), and the autopilot path (`main.js:357`) skips it. At touchdown the dial says
   **ROLL 177°** next to ATT "0° 0°" with the car on its wheels (`desk-7-touchdown.jpg`). It's the
   accumulated roll under an attitude ball. Over the row, ATT says R16° and the dial says 192°.
3. **The livestream's channels are still missing (0018, P0, sharpened).** #36 fixed the phone
   layout (every cell is inside 390 px). It did not add pitch: the ATT cell is the landing
   predictor's angle, which folds at ±90° ("R180° +56°" on the climb; the livestream read 170°).
   N₂ is a percentage with no kg, bar or regulator state. LOAD is still the per-frame filter.
4. **Tyre load sensitivity is unsourced and mild (0021, P1).** This car runs its tyres at 1.6–2.9 g
   of load, so how μ and cornering stiffness scale with load matters more here than on any road car.
   μ drops 7 % per unit of normalised load, cornering stiffness grows linearly with load, and
   `spec.js` gives no source for either. Published Magic Formula fits saturate cornering stiffness
   and usually carry a larger μ term. The builder must cite the exact table.
5. **On the phone the telemetry block sits mid-screen over the car during the carry**
   (`phone-5-over-row.jpg`: the strip spans y 268–366 px and the hero car is drawn behind
   "DAMAGE · INTACT"). This is noted, not ticketed: it's layout, and 0018 will move that block anyway.
   0018's builder should keep the car's screen band clear at 390×844.
6. **Still open from 03:45:** the kickflip hover (0014), isothermal tanks (0016), the Wall start
   (0019), the braking peak at 3.3 m against 6.5 m. None of them shipped this cycle, so nothing new
   to measure.

## What is now right (with evidence)

- **Phone telemetry is on the screen.** At 390×844 the strip's cells span x 90–374 px. SPD, ALT,
  SINK, LOAD, N₂, ATT and FAN are all readable through the flight (`phone-2-climb.jpg` …
  `phone-7-touchdown.jpg`). The phone part of 0018's first criterion passes.
- **0015 (relaxation length) cost nothing measurable.** Reference scores are unchanged (94 on
  production), 32/32, 54/54, cue followers 95/100, 0–60 0.67 s, and `gtrace`/`fan` tests are green.
  The touchdown side force now builds over σ instead of arriving at once (unit-tested). That is the
  right physics and invisible to the gates, which is what a transient should be.
- **The crash reads as a crash.** Throttle only, no LAND: the car goes off the lip and
  lands on its roof with sparks, dust and torn parts. The sensor reads 66 % WRECKED and LOAD
  shows 6.3 g in red (`desk-8-crash.jpg`).
- **First load:** FCP 1.2 s, LCP 2.1 s, 1.49 MB, own origin only.
