### 1. The privacy page says what production doesn't do (0155, P0)

`public/privacy/index.html:30`, live at `/coldgas/privacy` since #255, reads: *"Cold Gas has no accounts, no ads and
no tracking … Nothing goes to an advertising network, an analytics company or a data broker."* The App Store listing
will link to it.

- **Analytics on the page itself:** at 390×844 the page requests **`static.cloudflareinsights.com`**. That's
  Cloudflare Web Analytics, injected by the zone.
  - The Worker adds `Cache-Control: no-transform` only to `/coldgas/` and `/coldgas/index.html`
    (`src/worker/index.js:539-542`, `PAGES`).
  - `curl -I` on `/coldgas/privacy` shows no `cache-control` at all, and the game's HTML shows
    `public, max-age=0, must-revalidate, no-transform`.
  - So this is the one page that loads the beacon, and it's the page that says there's none.
- **Ads on the web game:** `GET /ads/api/config?app=coldgas` answers `"enabled": true`, with the title slot
  `"source": "network"` (AdSense `ca-pub-…`, ad unit 7120412190, `"test": true`).
  - `src/ads.js` loads `/ads/v1/ads.js` everywhere off localhost, and that loader writes
    `pagead2.googlesyndication.com/pagead/js/adsbygoogle.js`.
  - The 11-04 critique saw `pagead2.googlesyndication.com`, `googleads.g.doubleclick.net` and
    `ep1.adtrafficquality.google` on the iPad title. Today's 25 s iPad title loaded only `ads.js` and
    `/ads/api/config` (the frame wasn't placed), but the config makes ads a live path on the web.
- **Why it ranks first:** "nothing goes to an advertising network" is false for the web game as configured, and
  "no analytics company" is false for the page that says it. The ads are allowed (CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md §3.3); a privacy
  page that denies them isn't. The wording is the operator's (#255), so 0155 asks for the true words and for the
  beacon to stop, and leaves the phrasing to them.
