# Lessons

Mistakes already paid for. Read before working.

- **Loft winding.** `loft()` in src/render/cars.js must wind (a, b, c) so normals face out; inverted winding back-face-culls every body and lights the inside of the far wall (looked like black slabs).
- **No MSAA HalfFloat render target.** A multisampled HalfFloat composer target rendered pure black on some GPU paths; we use SMAA after the OutputPass instead.
- **Grounded attitude jets.** The flight computer must not fire on the wheels, or it damps suspension bounce with air and sheds mist on the start line.
- **Level-controller signs.** World-up in body frame: roll correction is `-u.x`, pitch correction is `+u.z`. Both were once flipped and the car tipped itself over on landing.
- **Free air without thrusters.** A tall kicker let a fast car clear the lineup with no thrust at all; the ramp is 1.2 m and no-trick jumps are capped.
- **aurora-field `fromText` threshold is 0–1.** Passing 128 matched no pixels and threw, which stalled the theatre. Every visual step is now wrapped in `safe()`, and a hard deadline guarantees the words land.
- **Fast models outrun the show.** The edge model can finish before the theatre reaches COMPUTE, so tokens are queued and played through COMPUTE at their recorded pace. The counters keep the real timings.
- **Gemma 4 on Workers AI thinks by default.** Without `chat_template_kwargs: { enable_thinking: false }` it spends 800 tokens reasoning and returns no answer within 400.
- **Local e2e in the cloud sandbox (2026-10-20).** `@playwright/test` 1.64 looks for `chromium_headless_shell-1248`, but the sandbox ships `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194`. Don't `playwright install`; run e2e through a throwaway, uncommitted config that spreads `playwright.config.js` and sets `use.launchOptions.executablePath: '/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194/chrome-linux/chrome'`. The full suite then runs locally in about a minute.
- **Engines disagree on transcendental functions.** The same input log scored 77.3 in Node, 76.0 in workerd and 71.3 in production, because Math.sin/atan2/hypot aren't required to be bit-identical and the physics amplified the difference. The fix is `src/sim/dmath.js` (fdlibm kernels using only +, −, ×, ÷ and √). Node, Chromium, WebKit and workerd are now bit-identical.
- **+x is the car's LEFT.** In a right-handed frame with +y up and +z forward, the driver's right is −x. The sim once called +x "right", and steer, roll and yaw all came out mirrored on screen; players called it unusable. Inputs are now driver-relative (+ = right), converted inside `vehicle.js`, and both the unit tests and the e2e tests check that right means right.
- **One fill of nitrogen is one stunt.** After the Wall the tanks are at about a third of their mass, and with blowdown (p ∝ m^1.375) the four lift jets barely match the car's weight. So the Show is one flight, not two stunts chained: the row sits under the ledge, and a ≈0.15 s rear-jet burn (≈20 kg) is the whole cost of carrying over it.
- **A parked car is a body, not a wall.** Each run owns its world (`createWorld`); contact forces go back onto the body they touch (`pushBody`), and stepWorld integrates them against parked-tyre friction. Never mutate the course's static boxes, or one run's shove leaks into the next (and into the server's replay).
- **Full throttle up the curl throws the car off the ledge at ≈24 m/s.** That's real and it's the player's call; the naive-show sweep (scripts/naive-show.mjs) shows the spread.
- **The HTML is served uncompressed.** `no-transform` (which keeps the zone's analytics beacon out) also stops Cloudflare compressing index.html, and the stylesheet is inlined there for first paint. Keep CSS lean, and keep the font faces latin-only. Nothing renders until `stage.ready` (src/render/scene.js), so anything added to the scene after first load is compiled on first sight: warm it with `renderer.compileAsync`.
- **Decals lose to a slope-scaled offset.** The approach strip used `polygonOffsetFactor: -1`; at grazing angles that pulls it toward the camera by more than the 4 mm the paint sat above it, so the paint flickered and mostly vanished. A layer on top must carry a stronger offset than the layer under it (`DECAL` in src/render/sliver.js), not just a lift. Measure flicker with `scripts/flicker.mjs` (heatmaps with `--heat=dir`) rather than by eye.
- **Sub-pixel emissive geometry flickers through the bloom.** The composer's HalfFloat target has no MSAA (see above), so a strip under a pixel wide is sampled on and off and bloom spreads every pop. Give such meshes an `aSpread` attribute and a `sliver()` material. Keep the lights opaque: alpha-blended LED strips measured worse (their blended edges sort against the moving car, mist and plumes).
- **Where a jet sits is how it flies.** Down-jets behind each wheel aren't symmetric about the CoM, so equal valves pitch the car; the mixer's TRIM cancels that and costs lift. The CoM position (an ESTIMATE) now sets the lift budget: source it before tuning landing feel.
- **Touching a spec file puts all of it under the flake guard (2026-10-20).** Changing `tests/e2e/smoke.spec.js` made CI repeat every test in it 5× in parallel. The BYOK test, where each click waits on the title's software-GL frames (2–5 s each), then ran past 60 s on 4 of 5 runs. It's just as slow on main. Slow-but-correct tests get `test.slow()` with a comment saying why. Never drop assertions, and don't move a new test to another file to dodge the guard.
- **Instanced quads need DoubleSide.** The crash sparks are camera-facing streaks built in the vertex shader. Their screen-space winding comes out the same for every spark, so with the default FrontSide every one was culled. The pool was full and nothing showed.
- **A spring is not a crash.** A fully elastic hull spring sank a hard hit 0.3–0.5 m into the ground and fired it back out at e ≈ 0.5–1. Bodywork yields and stays folded (vehicle.js HULL_YIELD). Keep the floor probes compliant: they are the skirts riding the deck, and making them stiff moved the Wall reference off the lip.
- **Anything async that draws over the stage needs an off switch.** The judges' theatre had no abort, so a restart mid-call left it playing over the next run. Long-lived overlays take an AbortSignal, and every flow change (startRun, toTitle) goes through closeOverlays().
