Keyboard
Ground: Up arrow throttle, Down arrow brake, Left and Right arrows steer, Space lift and let go, Shift boost
Air: Left and Right arrows roll, Up and Down arrows pitch, Q and E yaw, Space lift, F boost, Shift land
Any time: P pause, R retry, H home, G ghost, L pilot view, U units

Touch
One thumb (Assist): hold to climb, tap to let go, hold to land. The flight computer rolls you out and brakes.
Classic: a stick and pedals, with roll, boost and land by hand.

Gamepad
Supported. The pause screen lists the buttons for the pad in use.
